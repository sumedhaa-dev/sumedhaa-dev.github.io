## A curious beginning

I’ve always liked figuring things out.

Mathematics was one of the first places I found that feeling. There was something satisfying about taking a problem that seemed complicated and finding the pattern hiding inside it. Coding gave that instinct somewhere new to go. What started as an interest eventually became a career.

Six years into working in technology, I’ve moved through software engineering, data, cloud infrastructure, and now AI. I’ve worked on systems that move millions of files, built data pipelines and cloud platforms, spent far too much time thinking about infrastructure, and more recently, started exploring what happens when AI can actually work with the systems we build.

The technology keeps changing, which is probably part of what keeps me here. There is always another layer to understand, another problem hiding underneath the obvious one, another thing that could perhaps be made a little simpler.

## A life outside the screen

Work is a big part of my life, but it has never been the whole of it.

Dance has been with me since I was young. I learnt Bharatanatyam for years, left it for a while, and eventually came back to it. Coming back has been different. I notice things now that I probably didn't have the patience to notice then: the precision of a rhythm, the restraint behind an expression, the grace that comes only after doing something many, many times.

I love travelling for much the same reason I love learning anything new. There is something wonderful about being somewhere you don't quite understand yet. I like wandering through unfamiliar places, noticing how people eat, pray, work, celebrate and live, and slowly putting the pieces together.

Books give me that feeling too, only without having to leave home. I read across whatever happens to interest me, and I like that a good book can make an ordinary idea feel unfamiliar again.

## Still becoming

I’ve never been particularly good at choosing just one thing.

There are too many things I want to understand, places I want to see, things I want to make, and versions of myself I haven't met yet. I want to become better at my work without allowing it to become the only measure of a good life. I want to take my dance further. I want to keep travelling. I want to read more books than I have time for. I want to keep saying yes to experiences that scare me a little and leave me with a story afterwards.

I don't know exactly where all of that leads.

For now, I like that it is still unfolding.
